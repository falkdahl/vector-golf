# REQ-003: Vector Field Wind System

- **ID:** REQ-003
- **Title:** Vector Field Wind System
- **Priority:** Must Have
- **Type:** Functional
- **Status:** Draft
- **Related Plan Section:** Vector Field (core physics)

## Description
The map SHALL contain a 2D vector field that represents wind. The ball's velocity SHALL be continuously influenced by sampling the wind vector at the ball's current position each physics tick. The field must be more complex than a single direction (non-uniform, swirling/diverging) and SHALL be generated by random superposition of elementary potential-flow singularities (sources, sinks, doublets and vortexes) with no uniform (unary) flow. Every generated field SHALL contain at least one vortex or doublet strictly inside the playing field and at least one source and at least one sink placed exactly on the edge of the playing field.

## Rationale
User requirement: "wind should be more complex than just a single direction, the map should contain a vector field the ball has to navigate through". A grid-based field with bilinear interpolation satisfies complexity while remaining performant and pure-JS. Superposition of sources/sinks at the edge plus an interior vortex/doublet gives controllable inflow/outflow across the boundaries plus rotational/dipole features inside, with seeded reproducibility. Removing the uniform flow (which made fields boring) forces the field to be shaped only by singularities.

## Requirements

1. The field SHALL be defined as a regular grid over the canvas logical size (default `cols=20`, `rows=15` for 900x600 canvas, cell size ~45x40 px). Each cell stores `{x, y}` wind vector.
2. The field data structure SHALL be a 2D array `field[row][col] = {x, y}` in `src/vectorField.js`.
3. A function `getWindAt(worldX, worldY)` SHALL be exported from `src/vectorField.js` that:
   - Converts world coordinates to grid coordinates.
   - Performs bilinear interpolation of the 4 nearest cell vectors.
   - Clamps to field bounds.
   - Returns `{x, y}` in world units (pixels per second influence).
4. Generation — **Random Superposition of Edge Sources/Sinks + Interior Vortex/Doublet**: `createField` SHALL generate the field by superposing a random set of elementary flows seeded deterministically, **without any uniform (unary) flow**. Signature SHALL be:
   ```js
   createField(cols, rows, strength, seed, width, height, nSources, nSinks, nDoublets, nVortexes)
   // or createField(cols, rows, strength, seed, width, height, {sources, sinks, doublets, vortexes})
   ```
   The function **MUST take** at minimum `seed: number` and the four counts `nSources: number`, `nSinks: number`, `nDoublets: number`, `nVortexes: number` (order as above, or named fields). All four counts SHALL be integer `>=0`; if a count is `0` the corresponding singularity type is omitted, **except** the following mandatory constraints SHALL still be enforced regardless of the passed counts (or the call SHALL be considered invalid and the implementation SHALL coerce to satisfy them):
   - **At least one vortex or doublet inside**: `nVortexes + nDoublets >= 1` and at least one of those elements SHALL be placed strictly inside the playing field (`0 < ex < width && 0 < ey < height`, with margin `≥20px` from edge). If the caller passes `nVortexes=0 && nDoublets=0`, the implementation SHALL implicitly create one vortex or doublet inside (e.g., one vortex at random interior position) to satisfy the requirement.
   - **At least one source at the edge and at least one sink at the edge**: `nSources >= 1` and `nSinks >= 1` and at least one source and one sink SHALL be placed **exactly on the edge** of the logical canvas (`ex==0 || ex==width || ey==0 || ey==height`, within `≤1px` tolerance, sampled uniformly along the perimeter). If the caller passes `0` for either, the implementation SHALL implicitly create one at the edge. Additional sources/sinks beyond the mandatory one MAY also be placed on the edge (or, optionally, near-edge) but **no source or sink SHALL be placed strictly inside** (`0 < ex < width && 0 < ey < height`) and **no sink SHALL be placed outside** (`ex<0||ex>width||ey<0||ey>height`) — edge only. This replaces the previous "sinks outside" rule.
   `cols`, `rows`, `strength` (`WIND_STRENGTH` scale), `width`/`height` (canvas logical size) remain as before for grid geometry; `seed` controls all random choices. Defaults for backwards compat MAY be `seed=42, nSources=1, nSinks=1, nDoublets=1, nVortexes=1` (which already satisfies the mandatory constraints: 1 edge source, 1 edge sink, 1 interior vortex/doublet) if counts are omitted, but when counts are provided they SHALL be respected and coerced to satisfy the mandatory minima.
   - **Seeded randomness**: A deterministic PRNG (e.g., `mulberry32(seed)`) SHALL be created from `seed`. Every random choice (element position, strength, orientation) MUST derive from this PRNG so the same `seed` + same counts yields bit-identical field. The mandatory edge/interior placements SHALL also be derived from the same PRNG (e.g., first source is edge, first sink is edge, first vortex/doublet is interior, in that order).
   - **Element placement**:
     - **Sources at edge**: For each source, choose a random side (0:left `x=0`, 1:right `x=width`, 2:top `y=0`, 3:bottom `y=height`) via `rand()`, then sample the other coordinate uniformly along that side in `[0,width]` or `[0,height]`. Exactly on the edge (`x==0` or `x==width` or `y==0` or `y==height`). No margin inside.
     - **Sinks at edge**: Same edge sampling as sources, but for sinks. No sink SHALL be inside or outside — edge only. Verify with `ex==0||ex==width||ey==0||ey==height`.
     - **Vortexes / doublets inside**: Position `(ex, ey)` SHALL be sampled uniformly **inside** `[0,width] × [0,height]` with margin `20px` from edge (`x∈[20,width-20], y∈[20,height-20]`) to guarantee strictly inside. At least one vortex or doublet SHALL be inside; if both counts are zero, create one vortex inside.
     - **No unary flow**: No constant uniform vector `U` SHALL be added. `Vraw` SHALL be only superposition of singularities. The previous `unaryFlow` parameter SHALL be removed; any `unaryFlow` passed in an options object SHALL be ignored (or call SHALL throw a deprecation warning but still produce a field without unary).
   - Strength and orientation SHALL be sampled with ranges:
     - Sources: `sigma ∈ [0.7, 1.4]` (positive, at edge)
     - Sinks: `sigma ∈ [0.7, 1.4]` (applied as negative source, at edge)
     - Doublets: moment `mu ∈ [0.6, 1.3]`, orientation `theta ∈ [0,2π)` (inside)
     - Vortexes: circulation `Gamma ∈ [0.8, 1.6]` with random sign (`±` with equal probability) for clockwise/counter-clockwise (inside).
     Ranges are tunable but MUST be documented at top of `src/vectorField.js:5`.
   - **Element fields** (softened to avoid singularity, `a=35-45` softening radius, `eps=a²`):
     - **Source at edge** `(sx,sy)` with strength `S`: `dx=x-sx, dy=y-sy, r2=dx*dx+dy*dy+eps, contrib = S * (dx,dy) / r2` (outward from edge)
     - **Sink at edge**: same as source with `S = -sigma` (inward, field points toward edge sink)
     - **Vortex inside** `(vx,vy)` with `Gamma`: `r2=dx*dx+dy*dy+eps, contrib = Gamma * (-dy, dx) / r2` (tangential)
     - **Doublet inside** `(dx0,dy0)` with moment `mu` and angle `theta`: rotate `dx,dy` into doublet frame `dx' = cos theta*dx + sin theta*dy`, `dy' = -sin theta*dx + cos theta*dy`, `r2=dx'^2+dy'^2+eps, r4=r2*r2, local = mu * ( (dx'^2 - dy'^2)/r4, 2*dx'*dy'/r4 )`, rotate back `contrib = (cos theta*local.x - sin theta*local.y, sin theta*local.x + cos theta*local.y)`.
   - **Superposition**: For each cell centre `(cx,cy)`, raw vector `Vraw = Σ sources(edge) + Σ sinks(edge) + Σ doublets(inside) + Σ vortexes(inside)` (vector sum). No uniform flow SHALL be added.
   - The raw field SHALL then be scaled by `WIND_STRENGTH` factor at sampling time (see Req 5), but magnitudes after superposition MUST already satisfy **different strength at different locations** and **minimum force** (Req 6) before scaling interpretation.

5. A constant `WIND_STRENGTH` (default 80-120, tunable) SHALL scale the sampled vector before applying to velocity: `vel += wind * WIND_STRENGTH * dt`. **Wind SHALL have high acceleration**: effective wind acceleration `|wind|*WIND_STRENGTH` SHALL be strong enough that a ball slowed to <20 px/s by friction re-accelerates to >60 px/s within 0.5s when wind changes direction, so direction changes feel responsive and not sluggish.
6. **Minimum Force**: The effective wind force applied each tick SHALL never be less than 10% of max launch power. With `MAX_POWER=600`, the minimum effective force `|wind| * WIND_STRENGTH` SHALL be `>= 0.1 * MAX_POWER = 60` for every cell and every interpolated sample. Generation SHALL enforce this by scaling magnitudes so that `magnitude >= 0.1 * MAX_POWER / WIND_STRENGTH`. For `WIND_STRENGTH=80`, `magnitude >=0.75`; for `WIND_STRENGTH=60`, `magnitude >=1`. This ensures the ball never comes to rest and is continuously drifted by the field, while keeping vector arrows short (per REQ-004 arrow length is capped and not scaled with raw magnitude). For higher acceleration, use higher `WIND_STRENGTH` (e.g., 80-100) with correspondingly smaller magnitudes to keep visual size short but effective force high.
7. The field SHALL be deterministic per level load (seeded) so the challenge is consistent; given identical `seed, nSources, nSinks, nDoublets, nVortexes` the field SHALL be bit-identical on refresh. Different seeds or different counts SHALL produce distinct fields (verified by at least `>15%` vector difference at sampling points). **The field SHALL have different strength (magnitude) at different locations**, not only different direction: effective force (`|wind|*WIND_STRENGTH`) SHALL vary across cells while respecting the minimum force and keeping arrows short per REQ-004. Magnitudes SHALL vary by at least 10% between min and max (max ≥1.1× min), and at least two distant points (e.g., (100,100) vs (700,500)) SHALL differ by >8% in effective force.
8. No third-party noise libraries SHALL be used; implement inline math for PRNG and field formulas.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `src/vectorField.js:1` exports `createField`, `getWindAt`, and `WIND_STRENGTH`; `createField` signature is `createField(cols,rows,strength,seed,width,height,nSources,nSinks,nDoublets,nVortexes)` (or options object) **without** `unaryFlow` — calling with an 11th `unaryFlow` arg SHALL either ignore it or throw a clear deprecation, and the field SHALL be generated without unary (verify `getWindAt` with `nSources=0,nSinks=0,nDoublets=0,nVortexes=0` yields near-zero/fallback, not a uniform drift).
- [ ] **No unary**: `createField(20,15,90,42,900,600,2,1,1,1)` with and without an extra `unaryFlow={x:0.5,y:0}` argument produces identical fields (unary is ignored) or the extra arg is documented as ignored.
- [ ] **Edge sources/sinks**: For any generated field with `nSources>0`, at least one source satisfies `ex==0||ex==width||ey==0||ey==height` (within `≤1px`); for `nSinks>0`, at least one sink satisfies same edge condition. For 100 random seeds with `nSources=1,nSinks=1,nDoublets=1,nVortexes=1`, zero sources/sinks fall strictly inside (`0<ex<width && 0<ey<height`) and zero sinks fall outside (`ex<0||ex>width||ey<0||ey>height`). Verify via `getSourcePositions()`/`getSinkPositions()` or debug exposure.
- [ ] **Inside vortex/doublet**: For any generated field, at least one vortex or doublet satisfies `0<ex<width && 0<ey<height` with `≥20px` margin (i.e., `20≤ex≤width-20 && 20≤ey≤height-20`). For 100 random seeds with default `1,1,1,1`, every field has `nVortexesInside + nDoubletsInside ≥1`. If the caller passes `0,0,0,0`, the implementation coerces to create one interior vortex/doublet.
- [ ] `createField(20,15,90,42,900,600,1,1,1,1)` and `createField(20,15,90,43,900,600,1,1,1,1)` (different seed) produce distinct fields: sampling at (100,100) and (700,500) yields vector difference `>0.15` in `hypot(d)` or `>15°` angle diff for at least one point.
- [ ] Changing counts while fixing seed changes field: `createField(...,2,1,1,1)` vs `createField(...,0,0,0,0)` (coerced) yields different `getWindAt` results; adding a vortex introduces curl, adding a source at edge introduces radial outflow from edge.
- [ ] Source at edge contributes outward radial flow from edge (sample points just inside near source show vectors pointing away from edge), sink at edge contributes inward toward edge, vortex tangential, doublet dipole asymmetry — verified by unit tests with isolated element.
- [ ] `getWindAt(0,0)` and `getWindAt(890,590)` return distinct vectors (field is non-uniform) in both direction and magnitude for default `1,1,1,1`.
- [ ] Bilinear interpolation verified: sampling at cell center equals cell value; sampling at midpoint between two cells equals average of neighbors within 0.01 tolerance.
- [ ] Ball launched with identical angle/power but at two different map regions experiences different lateral deflection (>10px difference after 1s flight) due to field variance.
- [ ] Performance: `getWindAt` completes <0.05ms avg (measured over 1000 calls) even with max counts (e.g., 3+2+2+2=9 elements); `createField` completes <5ms for 20x15 grid.
- [ ] **Min Force**: For every grid cell and for 100 random interpolated samples, `hypot(wind.x, wind.y) * WIND_STRENGTH >= 0.1 * MAX_POWER` (60 when `MAX_POWER=600`). No zero wind exists, enforced after superposition scaling.
- [ ] **Varying Strength**: Field has different strength at different locations: effective force varies across grid (max ≥1.1× min) and sampling at (100,100) vs (700,500) yields difference >8%. Magnitudes are not uniform.
- [ ] Wind is strong enough that a ball with `vel=0` placed anywhere on the field begins moving within 1 tick and never remains stationary for >0.4s without drifting.
- [ ] Arrow size remains short per REQ-004 (≤16px) even with lowered min force; arrow length is decoupled from raw magnitude but still reflects relative strength.
- [ ] Determinism: calling `createField` twice with same `seed, nSources, nSinks, nDoublets, nVortexes, cols, rows, width, height, strength` yields identical `field` arrays (`JSON.stringify` equal).

## Notes
- Tunable: expose `cols`, `rows`, `WIND_STRENGTH`, softening `a`, and default counts at top of `src/vectorField.js:5` for playtesting. Example: `DEFAULT_SOURCES=1, DEFAULT_SINKS=1, DEFAULT_DOUBLETS=1, DEFAULT_VORTEXES=1` (already satisfies mandatory: 1 edge source, 1 edge sink, 1 interior vortex/doublet).
- Levels: `src/levels.js` `field` object SHOULD now include `sources, sinks, doublets, vortexes` counts (no `unaryFlow`) plus `seed` to allow per-hole variety (e.g., `field: {cols:20, rows:15, strength:90, seed:42, sources:1, sinks:1, doublets:1, vortexes:1}`); if counts are omitted, `createField` defaults apply and mandatory minima are coerced.
- Edge sampling helper `sampleOnEdge(width,height,rand)` that picks a random side (left/right/top/bottom) and a random `t∈[0,1]` along that side, returning `(x,y)` exactly on edge (`x==0` etc.). Verify with edge condition in tests.
- Superposition is linear; order of summation does not matter (`V = Σsources(edge) + Σsinks(edge) + Σdoublets(inside) + Σvortexes(inside)`). Softening radius `a` prevents blow-up near element centres: `r2 = dx*dx+dy*dy + a*a`. No unary.
- For playtesting, start with total elements 4-6 (e.g., `1+1+1+1`) to keep field readable; 9+ elements can create chaotic field. Edge sources/sinks create cross-field drift, interior vortex/doublet creates swirl.
