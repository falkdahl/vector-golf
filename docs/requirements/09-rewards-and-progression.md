## 1. Hole & Win Definition (extends `04-physics-and-collision.md`)

- Hole `hole={x,y,radius:14}` (12-16 tunable, black `fill #111` rim `2px #333`, optional flag). Position per `08-level-generation.md` (right side).
- **Win check every tick** (continuous, not at rest): `hypot(ball.pos-hole) < hole.radius + BALL_RADIUS` (any edge grazing counts, `dist < …` not `≤`).
  - On **non-final hole** win: **do NOT show Victory screen**. Auto-advance immediately: `vel=0`, `isMoving=false`, freeze, then consume spatial supply per `07-modifiers.md` §7, `modifiers=[]` via `syncModifiersToField()`, `currentHoleIndex++`, `holeAttempts=0` (keep `totalAttempts`), `isFreeShotActive=false`, regenerate `treasure` uncollected, `loadLevel(next)`, `gameState='AIMING'`, `saveProgress()`. No `WIN` state, no overlay, no `Next` button. HUD `Hole: N/M` updates (`M=LEVELS.length`).
  - On **final hole** win: `vel=0`, `isMoving=false`, freeze over hole, `gameState='WIN'`, show **Victory** overlay centered in canvas (DOM): `★★★` three stars + title `Course Completed!`, text `Total: Y` (per `05-input-and-states.md`, no hole/attempts-this-hole info), green `Continue` button with `Press R to continue` → `R` or `Continue` → `clearProgress()` removing active game + `mainMenuVisible=true`. Victory is **only** for the last hole of the course.

## 2. Hole Advancement

- **Non-final hole**: auto-advance on hole entry (no `Next` click, no `R` fallback needed) → **before clearing modifiers** consume spatial supply per `07-modifiers.md` §7 (`supply[type]=max(0,supply[type]-1)` per placed spatial modifier, `updateHotbarUI`+`saveProgress`), `freeShot` supply **not** consumed on win (only on free launch per `07` §5.2), then `modifiers=[]` via `syncModifiersToField()`, `currentHoleIndex++`, `holeAttempts=0` (but `totalAttempts` kept), `isFreeShotActive=false` (clear armed free shot), `treasureCollected=false` and regenerate `treasure` for next hole (fresh hole's treasure is uncollected, see `08` §4), `loadLevel(next)`, `gameState='AIMING'`. HUD `Hole: N/M` updates (`M=LEVELS.length`). Legacy `Next` button is not used for intermediate holes (Victory only on final hole).
- **Final hole**: same spatial supply consumption (then moot — `clearProgress()` resets supply to `{1,1,1,1,0}` with `rotate:1` for next new game), **do NOT reset to hole 1**; instead `clearProgress()` + `mainMenuVisible=true` (entry main menu, splash, no backdrop) keep `COURSES_KEY`/`bestTotal` intact; `bestTotal` for the course is updated **only here** (see §7 & `10-persistence-and-menus.md`).

## 3. Reward Triggers — Hole-Start (except hole 1) + Treasure Hit (single source; `05-input-and-states.md` defines HUD)

- **State**: `rewardPending:boolean`, `rewardOffered:string[]` (length 3), `rewardRerolled:boolean`, `rewardMenuVisible:boolean`, `treasure:{x,y,radius,isCollected:boolean,nearTreeId}` per hole (see `08` §4), plus `maxAttempts`/`holeAttempts`/`attemptsLeft` (see §4) and `supply.freeShot` / `isFreeShotActive` (see `07` §5.2).
- **On new game** (`resetGameAfterWin`, `startNewGameFromMain`, `endRun`, `clearProgress`, `initLevel(0)` with no save, page reload with no save): `rewardPending=false`, `rewardMenuVisible=false`, `rewardOffered=[]`, `rewardRerolled=false`, `maxAttempts=10`, `holeAttempts=0`, `attemptsLeft=10`, `supply={1,1,1,1,0}` (`amplify:1, nullify:1, flip:1, rotate:1, freeShot:0`), `isFreeShotActive=false`, **treasure uncollected** for hole 1 (`treasure.isCollected=false`), **no reward menu before first attempt on hole 1**. LoadLevel(0) generates one treasure near a random tree for hole 1 (see `08`). Player starts with **one rotate** (`rotate:1`).
 - **On each subsequent hole** (`currentHoleIndex>0`, `advanceHole`/`loadLevel(n>0)`): `rewardPending=true`, `holeAttempts=0` (so `attemptsLeft = maxAttempts`), `treasure` regenerated uncollected → `showHoleBanner()` (see `11-banners.md`) shows `Hole N` banner `1000ms` with same dim/style, then auto-transitions to `maybeShowRewardMenu()` which shows **Choose an Upgrade** **before first attempt** on that hole (before aim/charge/place) when banner hides and entering `AIMING`. `maxAttempts` persists (still `10`). `supply` carries over (spatial minus win consumption, `freeShot` minus launch consumption) and `isFreeShotActive` is cleared to `false` on hole advance. `rewardRerolled` reset to `false`. For `Hole 1` (`currentHoleIndex===0`) also show `Hole 1` banner `1000ms` then `AIMING` (no reward).
- **Treasure trigger**: Each hole has **exactly one treasure** (`level.treasure`, `treasureRadius 10-14`, default `12`, gold, see `03`/`08`). It is placed **near a tree** (`30±18px` from tree edge, deterministic per seed, see `08` §4). When the ball collides with the treasure (`hypot(ball.pos-treasure) < BALL_RADIUS + treasure.radius` and `!treasure.isCollected`), the treasure is **collected**: `treasure.isCollected=true`, `treasure` hidden, `rewardPending=true`, `maybeShowRewardMenu()` shows **Choose an Upgrade immediately** — even mid-flight (`FLYING`), freezing ball physics while menu is visible (like `AIMING`/`CHARGING`), not queued for next `AIMING`. If `rewardMenuVisible` or `WIN`/`GAME_OVER`/`pause`/`mainMenuVisible` is active, the pending reward waits until those are cleared, then shows. Collecting the treasure does **not** cost an attempt, does **not** increment `holeAttempts`/`totalAttempts`, and can only happen **once per hole** (until next hole generation). The treasure does not block movement, does not bounce, and does not count as obstacle/water.
 - **Increment**: on each launch, branching on Free Shot (see §4 & `07` §5.2):
  - **Auto-arm**: if `!isFreeShotActive && supply.freeShot>0 && getAttemptsLeft()<=1` at launch init, auto-set `isFreeShotActive=true` (see `05` §4 & `07` §5.2) so the last attempt becomes free.
  - If `isFreeShotActive===true && supply.freeShot >0`: **free launch** — do **not** increment `holeAttempts`/`totalAttempts`; do `supply.freeShot = max(0, supply.freeShot-1); if (supply.freeShot>0) keep `isFreeShotActive=true` (persist) else `isFreeShotActive=false`; `updateHotbarUI(); saveProgress();` (and visually keep/clear gold glow per remaining supply). No `attemptsLeft` change, no Game Over check. Ball still launches normally. Does **not** affect `rewardPending`. Free shot **persists** while supply remains.
  - Else (normal launch): do `holeAttempts++; totalAttempts++; attemptsLeft = max(0, maxAttempts - holeAttempts); updateAttemptsUI(); saveProgress();` **Do NOT check Game Over immediately** at launch — the last shot where `attemptsLeft` becomes `0` is allowed to finish (`FLYING` continues, `checkWin`/treasure/OOB/bounce still run) and can still win. If it then **fails** (`terrainHit`/`waterHit`/`edgeOut` while `attemptsLeft<=0`), `update()` shows **Game Over immediately** without `resetBall()` (see §4 and `05` §5). Free launches never trigger Game Over. Hole-start menu is shown at **next entry into `AIMING`** before next launch **only if still have attempts**; **treasure menu is shown immediately even mid-flight** (freezes physics) — allowed even on last flight when `FLYING` with `0` — otherwise if `FLYING` not applicable, next failure shows Game Over (see §5).
- `isFreeShotActive` toggle via `4` does not increment counters until the actual launch.

## 4. Max Attempts Hidden Counter `maxAttempts` + `attemptsLeft`

- `maxAttempts: integer ≥10`, hidden max (via `window.__getMaxAttempts` for tests), starts `10` on new game (`newGame`, `initLevel(0)`) and persists through death/`R`/hole advance. Derived `attemptsLeft = max(0, maxAttempts - holeAttempts)` is shown in HUD as `Attempts Left` (see `05`/`03`), not `freeShots`.
- **No deduction gating except Free Shot**: every normal `handleLaunch` is counted as above. A **Free Shot launch** (see §3) is the **only** way to make an attempt not decrease `Attempts Left` — it bypasses `holeAttempts` increment entirely and consumes `supply.freeShot` instead. After a normal launch, `holeAttempts` increments and `attemptsLeft` decrements by `1`; after a free launch, `attemptsLeft` is unchanged.
- **No `Max Attempts +5` reward**: the reward `Max Attempts +5` has been **removed**. `maxAttempts` is static `10` for the entire run (no reward increases it). Earlier docs mentioning `Max Attempts +5` are superseded. `maxAttempts` is still stored for forward compatibility and tests (`window.__getMaxAttempts` returns `10`), but no in-game action increments it. If legacy saves contain `maxAttempts >10`, clamp to that value on load (do not auto-decrease).
- **Game Over (immediate on last-attempt failure)**: if the **last counted attempt fails** ( `attemptsLeft <=0` and `gameState !== 'WIN'` after `attemptsLeft` became `0` ) without winning — i.e. `terrainHit`/`waterHit`/`edgeOut` detected in `update()` while `FLYING` with `attemptsLeft<=0` — trigger **Game Over immediately** without `resetBall()` (see `05` §5): `gameState='GAME_OVER'`, hide/clear `rewardMenuVisible`/`rewardPending`/`rewardOffered`, show Game Over screen, block input except return to menu. The last shot where `attemptsLeft` becomes `0` is **allowed to finish** (`FLYING` continues, `checkWin` can still win); only its **failure** (OB/water/edge) shows Game Over, not the launch itself. Fallback: starting a new attempt via `handleLaunch` while `attemptsLeft<=0` also shows Game Over if failure path was bypassed. Free launches never trigger Game Over because they don't decrement `attemptsLeft`; if `supply.freeShot>0` the last attempt is auto-armed free (see §3) so `attemptsLeft` never becomes `0`. `maxAttempts` itself is not decremented per attempt; only `holeAttempts` increments on counted launches.

## 5. Reward Menu — Random 3-of-6 Inside Canvas (rotate added, bouncy removed, Max Attempts +5 removed)

- Pool `POOL=['amplify','nullify','flip','rotate','freeShot','areaUp']` (6 types: `rotate` 90° CW added with refresh icon `↻` red `#e74c3c`, `Max Attempts +5` replaced by `freeShot` Supply +5, bouncy removed; earlier 5-pool `['amplify','nullify','flip','maxAttempts','areaUp']` and any 5-pool without `rotate` are legacy and superseded). Per trigger randomly pick **3 distinct** uniformly without replacement (`shuffle([...POOL]).slice(0,3)` via seeded `mulberry32` using `campaignSeed+rewardSeedCounter` per `12-campaign.md`, not `Math.random`), same 6-pool for reroll. Excluded types not shown that trigger.
- State per menu `rewardOffered` (current 3), `rewardRerolled=false` when freshly shown (`maybeShowRewardMenu` resets it), `rewardMenuVisible` blocks input. **Triggers are hole-start (hole index >0, before first attempt) and treasure hit** (one per hole near tree, see `08` §4); the 3-of-6 selection is identical for both triggers.
- **Inside-canvas canvas overlay** `drawRewardMenu(ctx,W,H,offered,hovered,rerolled,rerollHovered)` called from `render()` when `rewardMenuVisible`: full-canvas dim `rgba(0,0,0,0.55)` (no white card), title `Choose an Upgrade` 22px `700` white `stroke rgba(0,0,0,0.75) 5px` centered, three `90×110` buttons centered (`340×220` card) horizontally `gap 12`:
  - `amplify` border `rgba(230,126,34,0.9)` fill `rgba(230,126,34,0.28)` (hover `0.38`) icon `»` `#e67e22`; `nullify` `#3498db` `∅`; `flip` `#9b59b6` `⇄`; `rotate` red `↻` `#e74c3c` border `rgba(231,76,60,0.9)` fill `rgba(231,76,60,0.28)` label `Rotate` hint `+1 to supply` (refresh icon, distinct red not orange/blue/purple/gold); `freeShot` gold `★` `#f1c40f` label `Free Shoot` (also `Free Shot` acceptable) hint `Supply +5` / `+5 free shots` (replaces `Max Attempts +5`); `areaUp` amber `◯` `#f39c12` hint `+20% area`. All labels white with `stroke rgba(0,0,0,0.75) 4px` for contrast on green/dim; no `Total Attempts: N` subtitle.
- **Blocking**: while visible, aim/charge/launch/modifier `1`/`2`/`3`/`4` placement is ignored (no `rotate` placement via `4` while menu visible); only menu `1`/`2`/`3` (by **offered order** left-to-right, not fixed) or click on button rects selects; `07-modifiers.md` hotbar is visible but disabled underneath. Free Shot gold glow is hidden while reward menu is visible.
- **Selection** (idempotent, once per menu): click or `1`/`2`/`3` for `rewardOffered[0..2]`:
  - `amplify`/`nullify`/`flip`/`rotate` → `supply[t]++` once (rotate red `↻`),
  - `freeShot` → `supply.freeShot +=5` (exactly `+5`, `addToSupply('freeShot',5)`), clamped `≥0`; persists for remainder of run until consumed launch-by-launch (passive, no hotkey, shown in HUD as `Attempts Left: X (+Y)`),
  - `areaUp` → `areaUpgradeCount+=1` (`areaMultiplier=1+0.2*areaUpgradeCount`, retroactively grows spatial modifiers via `getEffectiveModifierRadius()`, see `07-modifiers.md` §4; base `BASE_MODIFIER_RADIUS` normative per `07-modifiers.md` — accept `54*` progression `64.8/75.6/...` if base `54`, or `108/126/...` if base `90`; additive not `1.2^n`),
  then `rewardPending=false; rewardMenuVisible=false; rewardOffered=[]`. Menu closes, normal `AIMING` resumes. Save via `saveProgress()`. Trees always bounce per `04-physics-and-collision.md:5`, no `bouncy`, no `Max Attempts +5`. For treasure-triggered menus, `treasure.isCollected` remains `true` (treasure stays hidden) after selection.

## 6. Re-roll — Once per Menu for 1 Counted Attempt (never free)

- While `rewardMenuVisible && rewardRerolled===false`, a **Re-roll button** `110×28` centered at `cardY+155` (below three cards) is shown: available fill `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` (hover `0.22`) border `rgba(255,255,255,0.85) 1.5px` icon `↻`, label `Re-roll` `12px 700` white, `(1 attempt)` `10px` `rgba(255,255,255,0.85)`, `[R]` hint. When `rewardRerolled===true` button is disabled (`0.06` fill, `0.35` border, `not-allowed`) or hidden. Hit-test via `getRewardRerollButtonLayout(W,H)`.
- Input: click on reroll button **or** `R`/`r` (`KeyR`) while menu visible and not yet rerolled → `rerollReward()` exactly once (hotkey is **`R`**, not `0`; `Digit0` is **not** a reroll shortcut):
  ```
  holeAttempts+=1; totalAttempts+=1; attempts=totalAttempts; attemptsLeft = max(0, maxAttempts - holeAttempts); updateAttemptsUI(); saveProgress();
  if (attemptsLeft <=0 && !WIN) { showGameOver(); /* hide reward, no new offer */ } else {
    rewardRerolled=true;
    rewardOffered=shuffle([...POOL]).slice(0,3);
  }
  // do NOT touch maxAttempts, do NOT consume freeShot
  // isFreeShotActive is forced false during reward menu (free launches not allowed while menu visible)
  ```
  Always costs a **counted** attempt (never a free shot, even if `isFreeShotActive` was true — menu blocks freeShot toggle). If it makes `attemptsLeft 0`, **Game Over is shown immediately** (no new reward, menu hidden, `showGameOver` clears `rewardMenuVisible`/`rewardPending`). Counts toward `maxAttempts`. Second `R`/click returns `false` (no cost, no change). After reroll the new `rewardOffered` is selectable via `1`/`2`/`3` **only if still have attempts**. `Digit0`/`Numpad0` does **not** trigger reroll.
- Re-reroll state is cleared on next fresh menu trigger (hole-start or treasure); `resetGameAfterWin`/`startNewGameFromMain`/`endRun`/`clearProgress` also clears `rewardRerolled`. Re-roll is blocked when not `rewardMenuVisible` (normal `R` stays `resetBall`); when `WIN`/`GAME_OVER` overlay visible re-roll is blocked. The re-roll attempt **delays** next reward and counts toward `maxAttempts`.

## 7. Persistence Interactions & Course Records

- Only **full course completion** (final-hole `WIN`) updates `bestTotal` per course (`10-persistence-and-menus.md`); `End Run` and `Game Over` do not.
- Counters and `supply` (now `{amplify,nullify,flip,rotate,freeShot}` with `rotate:1, freeShot:0` on new game)/`maxAttempts`/`holeAttempts`/`attemptsLeft`/`isFreeShotActive`/`areaUpgradeCount`/`reward…`/`treasure.isCollected`/`modifiers`/`aimAngle` are part of `STORAGE_KEY` payload and re-saved after every launch/claim/reroll/placement/toggle/treasure-hit/advance (see `10-persistence-and-menus.md`). `maxAttempts` legacy reward and `bouncyBallCount` are removed (kept for compat). `freeShot` supply starts `0` (passive, shown in HUD `(+Y)`) and `rotate` starts `1`; legacy saves missing `freeShot`/`rotate` default to `0`/`1` respectively (or `0` then auto-migrated to `1` if missing `rotate` and `amplify` exists). Treasure re-generated per hole; `isCollected` persists per hole until hole advance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] New game hole 1 (course hole 1): no reward menu before first attempt, one uncollected treasure near a tree (`level.treasure`, `radius 10-14`, `dist to nearest tree 12-45`, not overlapping tree/water/green/tee, on fairway/rough, `terrainZoneAt` valid, treasure visible gold). `maxAttempts=10`, `holeAttempts=0`, `attemptsLeft=10` shown as `Attempts Left: 10 (+0)` (or `Attempts Left: 10` when zero, but with `+5` after freeShot reward e.g. `Attempts Left: 10 (+5)`), `supply.freeShot=0` (passive) and `supply.rotate=1`.
- [ ] Hole 2..N (any hole except first of course): `maybeShowRewardMenu()` shows **Choose an Upgrade** before first attempt (`currentHoleIndex>0`, `holeAttempts===0`, `treasure.isCollected===false` treasure still hidden until hit, `rewardPending===true` before entry, `rewardMenuVisible` true in `AIMING`, blocks launch). Selecting an upgrade closes menu and enables `AIMING` to shoot. Hole 1 never shows this hole-start menu.
- [ ] Treasure: each hole has exactly one `treasure` (`{x,y,radius, isCollected:false}`) placed **near a tree** (random tree from `treesOnFairway` or any tree, offset `18-42px` from tree edge at random angle via seeded `rand()`, not overlapping tree `dist >= tree.r + treasure.r + 4`, not in water/OB/green/tee, `dist(tee,treasure) > 40` and `dist(hole,treasure) > green.r+20`). When `hypot(ball.pos-treasure) < BALL_RADIUS + treasure.radius` and `!isCollected`, `isCollected=true`, `rewardPending=true`, `maybeShowRewardMenu()` shows **immediately even in `FLYING`** (freezes ball, shows Choose an Upgrade). Hitting already-collected treasure does nothing.
- [ ] Reward from hole-start and treasure share same 6-pool `amplify/nullify/flip/rotate/freeShot/areaUp` (3 distinct per menu, `1/2/3` by offered order). Grants: `amplify/nullify/flip/rotate` `+1 supply` (rotate red `↻`), `Free Shot Supply +5` → `supply.freeShot+=5` (passive, shown as `Attempts Left: X (+Y)`), `areaUp` `areaUpgradeCount+1` → `effectiveRadius` additive (`BASE*1.2/1.4/1.6`…), retroactively grows spatial modifiers. No `Max Attempts +5`/`bouncy`.
- [ ] Normal launches increment `holeAttempts`/`totalAttempts` and decrement `attemptsLeft`; **free launch** (`isFreeShotActive && supply.freeShot>0`, including auto-arm) does **not** change `holeAttempts`/`totalAttempts`/`attemptsLeft` but does `supply.freeShot--` exactly once and **persists** `isFreeShotActive=true` while `supply.freeShot>0` after decrement (only clears when supply reaches `0`); `Game Over` is **immediate on last-attempt failure**: after `attemptsLeft` becomes `0`, ball finishes last flight and can still win (`Attempts Left: 0` during flight); if that flight then hits `terrainHit`/`waterHit`/`edgeOut`, `GAME_OVER` appears immediately without `resetBall()` to tee. Without win and without free shot, `GAME_OVER` screen with only `Return to Main Menu` does `clearProgress()` and returns to entry (no `Continue`), hiding any reward instantly. Fallback: `handleLaunch` while `attemptsLeft<=0` also shows Game Over. Free launches never trigger Game Over. Treasure hit never increments attempts. When `getAttemptsLeft()<=1` at launch init and `supply.freeShot>0`, free shot auto-arms.
- [ ] Tree hit always bounces (unlimited), water `z>5` flies over. Treasure hit does **not** bounce nor kill; it only collects.
- [ ] Re-roll button shown when `rerolled===false`; `R`/click once increments `holeAttempts`/`totalAttempts` (counted, not free, and decrements `attemptsLeft`) but keeps `maxAttempts`/`supply.freeShot` unchanged; if `attemptsLeft` becomes `0` shows **Game Over immediately** (hides reward, no new offer), otherwise replaces offer (new 3 from new 5-pool) and disables button; second `R` no-ops. `Total` at second reward is `11` counted if one reroll taken after hole-start (reroll costs one of the `maxAttempts`). Free shot cannot be used for reroll cost.
- [ ] No white card; dim `0.55` behind high-contrast white-with-stroke buttons; HUD still underneath shows `Attempts Left`. Treasure rendered as gold chest/star on `game` canvas above trees, below ball, hidden after collection until next hole.
