# 13 — Tutorial Course (The Proving Grounds)

- **ID:** 13-tutorial
- **Supersedes:** 07-level-generation.md (3-hole generation), 09-persistence-and-campaign.md (course naming, stage generation), 10-progression.md (starting overlay), 11-cutscenes.md (intro chain), 12-banter.md (run-start sequence) — only for `holeCount===4` (The Proving Grounds)
- **Type:** Functional / Content

## 1. Course Identity

- The `4`-hole course is a **fixed tutorial** named exactly `The Proving Grounds` (`course.name === "The Proving Grounds"`). It is not generated from `campaignSeed`; its holes are deterministic and identical for all playthroughs and campaign seeds.
- In `COURSES_KEY`, the staged entry for `holeCount===4` (legacy `3`) always has `name:"The Proving Grounds"` and `holes` from `generateTutorialLevels()` (see §2). Regeneration of campaign still produces the same `The Proving Grounds` (not name-randomized). Other stages (6/9/18) remain deterministic from `campaignSeed` as before.
- `generateCampaignCourse(3, ...)` and `generateCourse(3, ...)` must return the fixed 4-hole tutorial when `holeCount===3` regardless of seed/options (legacy alias: `holeCount 3` maps to 4-hole Proving Grounds).
- Legacy saves with `name==="3-hole Trial"` or `"The 3-Hole Trial"` shall be migrated to `"The Proving Grounds"` on `loadCourses()` and `holeCount` migrated to `4`.

## 2. Level Definitions

### Hole 1 — Unary Headwind Straight (Heavy Wind, Centered)

- **Treasure:** none (`level.treasure === null` or absent, `level.treasures` empty). No chest rendered or hittable.
- **Terrain:** straight fairway (`shape:"I"`) without obstacles (`obstacles:[]`, `waterHazards:[]`). Spine is straight tee→hole. `widthFairway`/`widthRough` per Easy tier but no hazards to block corridor.
- **Positions:** `tee` centered (`x 320, y 360`), `hole` centered-right (`x 940, y 360`) so distance ≈ 620 (shortened to compensate for heavy wind and centered to middle of screen, not left-biased `90→700`). Uses same `LOGICAL_W/H` (1280×720). Fairway is horizontally centered (`(tee.x+hole.x)/2 ≈ 630`).
- **Wind field:** **unary operator only, heavy headwind**. `field.unaryFlow` is a constant vector opposing the tee→hole direction (headwind, `x negative, y ~0` with magnitude ≈ `1.95` field units → `≈ -180 px/s` effective), `field.sources=0, sinks=0, doublets=0, vortexes=0`. No source/sink/vortex/doublet. `WIND_STRENGTH=180` applies. Tuned so a full-power shot (`power=600`, 100% charge) **just fails to reach the hole** (short by ~30-60px), while placing a **liquifier** bubble covering any part of the fairway lets the same shot reach and win. `vectorField.createField` must support `unaryFlow` and allow zero components when it is present.
- **Difficulty:** `tier:"easy"` `shape:"I"` `treesOnFairway:0` `waterOnFairway:0`.

### Hole 2 — Unary Side Wind Straight (No Obstacles, Strong Crosswind, Slightly Shortened)

- **Treasure:** none (`level.treasure === null`, `level.treasures` empty or single null).
- **Terrain:** straight fairway (`shape:"I"`) **without any obstacles** (`obstacles:[]`, `waterHazards:[]`), `widthFairway`/`widthRough` per Easy tier but no hazards. Slightly shortened full-width: `tee` at `x 120, y 360`, `hole` at `x 1080, y 360` (distance ≈ 960, was `40→1240` 1200 px, then `80→1160` 1080 px, now `120→1080` — “slightly more” shortened). Uses `LOGICAL_W/H` (1280×720).
- **Wind field:** **unary operator only, strong side wind bottom→top**. `field.unaryFlow` is `{x: ~0, y: -1.8 to -2.2}` (negative y = bottom to top, magnitude ≈ 1.8-2.2 field units → ≈ 320-400 px/s effective crosswind), `field.sources=0, sinks=0, doublets=0, vortexes=0`. No other components.
- **Difficulty:** `tier:"easy"` `shape:"I"` `treesOnFairway:0` `waterOnFairway:0`.

### Hole 3 — Same as Hole 2 + 3 Treasure Chests (Deflector/Rotator/Magnifier)

- **Treasure:** three (`level.treasures` array length 3, `level.treasure` is first for backward compat) spread along fairway at `t≈0.25,0.5,0.75` (`x≈360,600,840, y≈360±14`, `terrainZoneAt==='fairway'`, radius 12 each, not overlapping obstacles, not near tee/green masks, `isCollected` individually). All three chests are on fairway mid-line, not near trees. This replaces the previous single mid treasure.
- **Reward per chest:** each chest when collected (`checkTreasureHit` + `collectTreasure` on that chest) triggers a **special tutorial reward menu** with exactly three options: `deflector`, `rotator`, `magnifier` (`rewardOffered=['deflector','rotator','magnifier']` in any order, 3 cards, no Reroll? Reroll still disabled as tutorial? For hole3 chests, Skip is not allowed — mandatory, `closeRewardMenuWithoutReward` no-op, `Escape` does not close, `#reward-skip-button` hidden). All chests contain one deflector, one rotator and one magnifier (i.e., the offer is those three). Distinct from hole-start rewards.
- **Terrain/Wind:** identical to Hole 2 (same `tee {120,360}/hole {1080,360}/spine/widthFairway/widthRough` and same unary side wind bottom→top). No obstacles (`obstacles:[]`, `waterHazards:[]`). Geometry bit-identical to Hole 2 except for the 3 chests.
- **Difficulty:** `tier:"easy"` `shape:"I"` `treesOnFairway:0` `waterOnFairway:0`.

### Hole 4 — Same as Hole 3 (Full-Width Side Wind with Treasure)

- **Treasure:** one at mid fairway as Hole 3's middle chest (`x≈600, y≈360, radius 12`, `isCollected` per chest) — Hole 4 is bit-identical to Hole 3 in terrain/wind/treasure layout (full-width `120→1080` side wind, no obstacles). Treasure is not used for hole-start reward; hole-start reward is the passive triple (see §3). Chest on hole4, when taken, **does** show a reward (fix: previously showed none) — it shows the passive triple `fieldExtender/powerCell/freeShot` (same as hole-start reward) and **does not** show `Hole 4` banner after.
- **Terrain:** straight fairway `shape:"I"` **without any obstacles** (`obstacles:[]`, `waterHazards:[]`), `tee {120,360} hole {1080,360}`, `widthFairway 110/widthRough 190`, `LOGICAL_W/H` 1280×720, `noiseSeed` deterministic, `warpScale 0.008/warpStrength 8`.
- **Wind field:** **unary side wind bottom→top** as Hole 2/3 (`field.unaryFlow {0,-2.0}, sources=0, sinks=0, doublets=0, vortexes=0`). Not standard Easy `1,1,1,0`; it is same as Hole 3 for consistent side-wind tutorial.
- **Difficulty:** `tier:"easy"` `shape:"I"` `treesOnFairway:0` `waterOnFairway:0`.

## 3. Run Flow & UI Changes (only for The Proving Grounds)

### Start of Run
- **No Starting Items overlay** (`#starting-items-overlay` shall remain `.hidden` for `The Proving Grounds`; `isStartingItemsVisible()===false`). Player skips the “Choose 2 starting items” screen.
- **Starting bag:** player starts Hole 1 with exactly **one `liquifier`** (`golfbag = [{type:"liquifier"}, null, null, null]`, `supply.liquifier===1`, others 0). No other modifiers, no passive stacks at start.
- **Opening banter:** immediately after `handleCoursePlay` loads hole 1 preview (before `showHoleBanner(0)`), play tutorial controls banter (`controls-first`) over the loaded hole. This replaces the generic `playRunStartBanter` shuffle for the tutorial. On **replay** the same banter replays. Content: Caddy describes controls — aim with arrows, shoot with space, reset with r, pause with escape, place items with left-click, pick up items with right click, use hotkeys to select an item in the bag.
- **Infinite attempts & hidden HUD/score:** on `The Proving Grounds` the player has **infinite attempts** (`getAttemptsLeft()` effectively Infinity, never `0`, `isLastAttemptForReset()` always `false`, `Game Over` never triggered). **HUD** `#hud-attempts` (`Attempts Left: X`) and `#hud-total` (`Points: N`) shall be hidden (`display:none` / `.hidden`) while `isTutorialActive()` — no "Attempts Left" is shown on tutorial holes (report: pressing R should not visibly decrease Attempts Left). **Points** are not accumulated and **end-hole summary overlay** `#coin-summary-overlay` (`Hole Complete` / `Run Complete` with `+X points`) shall never be shown on tutorial (see Hole 1→2 etc.). `holeAttempts` still increments internally for help-banter triggers (3/5/10) but is not displayed.

Trigger once (flag `tutorialHole1Liquifier3Shown`).

Your Liquifier will remove the head wind…”). Trigger once. Both banters are short (1 line), block input like any banter, and reset on new run/hole advance.

## 4. Persistence

- `CUTSCENE_SEEN_KEY` gating applies to `first-restock`. Once seen, subsequent `handleCoursePlay` for `The Proving Grounds` does not play it; banner/banter still plays. `localStorage.clear()` / `regenerateCampaign()` clears seen, so next fresh run shows cutscene again.
- No `isTutorialRun` flag required beyond detecting `activeCourse.name==="The Proving Grounds"`; tutorial banters are not subject to the generic `BANTER_STATE_KEY` shuffle — they are fixed ids per step. `isTutorialActive()` helper shall be `course.name==="The Proving Grounds"` regardless of holeCount.
- Infinite attempts flag is tied to `isTutorialActive()` and hides `#hud-attempts`/`#hud-total` and suppresses `#coin-summary-overlay`. `getLevelTreasures(level)` helper supports 3 chests on hole3.
