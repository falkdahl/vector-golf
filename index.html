How to Play

Get the ball into the hole in as few attempts and strokes as possible. Wind vectors push the ball while it flies; wind varies per hole and course via a vector field. Place modifiers (Amplify, Nullify, Flip) before shooting to alter wind locally. Press 4 to arm Free Shot – gold glow over ball shows next attempt is free and won't decrease Attempts Left. Each hole tracks Attempts Left; total across the course is your score (fewest wins). Low record per course is saved.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — Aim
  • Space — Hold to charge power, release to shoot
  • Click — Place selected modifier
  • Right-click — Remove modifier
  • 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 — Select Amplify / Nullify / Flip / Free Shot
  • H — Toggle wind visualization
  • R — Reset ball
  • Escape — Pause
